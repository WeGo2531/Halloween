★ Right in West Chicago

Fall Festival & Pumpkin Patch

Sonny Acres Farm, 29W310 North Ave, West Chicago, IL · free admission & parking, pay per attraction

Pumpkin patch, farm store, and Sweet Shoppe, live DJs and entertainment, and a full food and drink lineup including hot and spiked apple cider. Haunted Hayride and Haunted Barn run select nights Oct 2–30 as a separate ticketed after-dark experience.

Free admission Pay-per-ride attractions

Worth it if: you go on a weekday. Reviews are genuinely split — longtime visitors love the animals, hayride, and rides, but weekend crowds and parking get called chaotic (2,000+ cars some days), and individual attractions run $6–10 each, which adds up fast for a family. Tuesday–Friday is the sweet spot.

Source: sonnyacres.com · Yelp reviews

Pumpkin Fest

9:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily · Cosley Zoo, 1356 N. Gary Ave, Wheaton, IL · regular zoo admission (free for kids 0–17 & Wheaton Park District residents)

Thousands of pumpkins line the zoo's front lawn all month, alongside the zoo's regular animal exhibits. Pumpkins, gourds, cider, and cornstalks for sale.

Free for kids

Worth it: this is a low-key, low-crowd option compared to the bigger farms — a real working zoo rather than a carnival, so it's a gentler pace for younger kids.

Source: enjoyillinois.com

Pumpkin FunFarm

Kuipers Family Farm, 1N318 Watson Rd, Maple Park, IL · ~1 hr from West Chicago · $7–$15/person depending on activity

A full day of outdoor fall activities: corn maze, jumping pillow, mountain slide, farm animals, pig races, apple blaster, and the U-Pic Apple Orchard (more than 45 varieties) still going alongside the pumpkin harvest. The Orchard Shop & Bakery is free to browse without a ticket.

Ticketed Corn maze U-pick apples

Worth it: consistently well-reviewed for atmosphere, food (apple cider doughnuts get singled out often), and the sheer amount to do — the recurring complaint is that it's pricey once you add up apple picking, the corn maze, and activity tickets separately.

Source: kuipersfamilyfarm.com · Tripadvisor reviews

U-Pick Apples & Corn Maze

County Line Orchard, 200 S County Line Rd, Hobart, IN · ~1.5–2 hrs from West Chicago · free for kids 2 & under, apples charged by weight, maze/train extra

A big, well-known destination on the Indiana side — antique tractor rides, an animal barnyard, a 10-acre corn maze (some years themed around sports moments, like a Caitlin Clark tribute maze), a "moo choo" train, and a bakery known for its apple cider doughnuts.

Large-scale Corn maze Bakery

Worth it: reviews are genuinely mixed the bigger it's gotten — longtime visitors call it a beloved yearly tradition with great cider and doughnuts, but recent reviews mention it feeling more crowded and commercial than it used to, with some inconsistency in food availability late in the day.
