Fall Festival & Pumpkin Patch

Sonny Acres Farm, 29W310 North Ave, West Chicago, IL · free admission & parking, pay per attraction

✓ Free admission & parking, pay per attraction

Pumpkin patch, farm store, and Sweet Shoppe, live DJs and entertainment, and a full food and drink lineup including hot and spiked apple cider. Haunted Hayride and Haunted Barn run select nights Oct 2–30 as a separate ticketed after-dark experience.

Free admission Pay-per-ride attractions

Worth it if: you go on a weekday. Reviews are genuinely split — longtime visitors love the animals, hayride, and rides, but weekend crowds and parking get called chaotic (2,000+ cars some days), and individual attractions run $6–10 each, which adds up fast for a family. Tuesday–Friday is the sweet spot.

Source: sonnyacres.com · Yelp reviews
